🏠 Home Hub Setup

Follow these steps once to connect your home iPad and your phone so they stay in sync in real time.

📋 Step-by-step (takes about 10 minutes)

1

Open firebase.google.com on your computer or phone. Sign in with a Google account (Gmail is fine).

2

Click Go to Console → Add project. Name it anything (e.g. Home Hub). Disable Google Analytics when asked (you don't need it). Click Create project.

3

In the left menu click Build → Realtime Database → Create Database. Choose any location, then select Start in test mode and click Enable.

4

Go to Project Settings (gear icon top-left) → scroll down to Your apps → click the </> (web) icon → register app (any name) → you'll see a block of code. Find these values: apiKey, authDomain, databaseURL, projectId.

5

Paste each value into the boxes below, then tap Save & Open App. Do this on both the iPad and your phone — same values both times.
